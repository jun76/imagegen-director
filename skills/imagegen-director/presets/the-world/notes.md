# Writing notes for the-world

Theme-specific notes on top of guides/prompt_writing.md.

- The scope is deliberately broad, from abstract concepts to imagined worlds. The leaf subject must
  stay recognizable in every medium, including micrographs, screenshots and children's drawings.
- Abstract leaves (void, eternity, consciousness) need a visual stand-in: light, space, symbols.
- Cosmic subjects in everyday media: show them on an observatory monitor, a planetarium dome, a
  printed chart, or through a telescope, rather than claiming a snapshot of a galaxy.
- Micrograph x macroscopic subject: show texture, cross-section, or the subject's material.
- region and era apply to people, activities, artifacts and symbols. Absorb contradictions:
  "office meeting" x "ancient" -> elders in council; "smartphone" x "medieval" -> a hand-held
  message tablet.
- Depict disability, ethnicity and body types respectfully and matter-of-factly.
- Conflict and crime: show aftermath, tension and traces, never gore. Zombies and demons stay
  cartoonish or costumed.
- Media phrasing used in the published set (rotate, do not repeat one formula):
  - DSLR photo: "professional photograph with shallow depth of field and crisp detail",
    "sharp photograph with smooth background blur and accurate color", ...
  - smartphone snapshot: "casual handheld snapshot with flat automatic exposure",
    "everyday candid snapshot with deep focus and punchy HDR-like processing", ...
  - security or dashcam still: "grainy surveillance video frame with a timestamp overlay",
    "wide fisheye driving-video frame seen over a vehicle hood".
